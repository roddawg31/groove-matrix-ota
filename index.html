Groove Matrix Recovery

Firmware 3.6.4
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or Chromebook.
  2. Connect the ESP32 directly by USB and keep the sign powered.
  3. Click Connect, select the CP210x/USB Serial device, then install.
  4. Do not unplug it until installation finishes and the sign restarts.

This writes the boot and application partitions while leaving the saved settings/NVS area out of the flash manifest. After installation, join Groove Matrix and use http://192.168.4.1/update for future offline phone updates.
